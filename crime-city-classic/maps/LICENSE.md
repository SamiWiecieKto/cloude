This derivative database preserves street nodes, street centerline geometry, road names, building outlines, parks and water polygons. Coordinates use a local metric projection and are rounded to one game unit (approximately one metre). Road widths are gameplay adaptations. Road access and tunnels are simplified; this is not a real-world navigation system.

The three files srodmiescie-data.js, buildings-1.js and buildings-2.js together form the complete machine-readable derivative database. They assign a JSON object to `window.WARSAW_MAP` and append the building arrays. It can be downloaded from the in-game atlas. Geographic license terms apply to these data, not automatically to independent game code or original audio.

Road/building/park source snapshot: Overpass timestamp 2026-05-31. Additional river multipolygons: OpenStreetMap API, September 2026. No commercial map tiles or imagery are used.
